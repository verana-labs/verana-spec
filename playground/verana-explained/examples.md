

# Run the demos

Download your favorite modified wallet to run the demos.

## Obtain an ECS Badge

Always verify the certified Organization name and data that is shown in the Proof-of-Trust card in your wallet before proceding.

- from Vesta
- from Zenith Repair
- from an Organization that is not an Authorized Repairer

## login as Repair employee with ECS-Badge

Always verify the certified Organization name and data that is shown in the Proof-of-Trust card in your wallet before proceding.

Try to log-in to Vesta portal with a badge:

- if you are a Vesta employees, you are recognized and have access as an employee.
- else if your ECS-Badge has been issued by an Organization that present a Authorized Repairer credential, then your access is granted, as Authorized Repairer employee
- else your access is Denied.

## Search the directory of Authorized Repairers

- view the list of all Organizations that present a Authorized Repairer credential and choose which company to contact for service

- limit to all Organizations that present a Authorized Repairer credential AND an ISO 9001 credential.
